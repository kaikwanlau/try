# data/figure_inputs/

Inputs that `5_figures/figure.py` uses for three panels and that are not part of the mesh data.
Copy them here once. Until then, figure.py leaves those panels as labelled blanks and draws the
rest of each figure normally.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `photo/C.pallidus.png` | Fig 1(a), the photograph of *Camarhynchus pallidus* (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0) |
| `raw_meshes/P2.InornataA.stl` | Fig 1(b), the raw scan mesh of *Pinaroloxias inornata* |
| `prior_remeshing/C.PallidusA.stl` | Fig S1, left column: the remeshing of the earlier studies |
| `prior_remeshing/C.flaveolaA.stl` | Fig S1, left column: the remeshing of the earlier studies |
